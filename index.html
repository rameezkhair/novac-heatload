Methodology

How the heat balance method works

NOVAC follows the ASHRAE heat balance method (Fundamentals, Ch. 18). Rather than looking up tabulated factors, it balances the heat flowing through every surface and the room air, hour by hour.

  1. Outside surface balanceEach wall, roof and window takes in sun and gains or loses heat to outdoor air, the sky and the ground. The outdoor conditions are the design day for the 21st of each month.
  2. Conduction through the layersHeat moves through each layer of the build-up, with its own conductivity and thermal mass. That mass delays and softens the afternoon peak.
  3. Inside surface balanceEach inside face exchanges long-wave radiation with the other surfaces and absorbs sunlight through the glass and radiant heat from people, lights and equipment.
  4. Room air balanceThe cooling load is the convection from every surface, plus the convective part of the internal gains, plus infiltration.

A repeating design day

Each design day is solved in its settled, repeating state, with no warm-up error. This gives the same result as running hourly balances until one day matches the next.

Peak hour and month

All 24 hours of all 12 months are calculated for every room. NOVAC reports each room at its own peak and the building at its coincident peak.

Components that add up

The model is linear, so the peak splits exactly into walls, roof, glass, people, lighting, equipment and infiltration. Fresh air and margins are then added for plant sizing.

FAQ

Common questions

Is NOVAC free?

Yes. NOVAC runs in your browser with no licence, no installation and no sign-up. Pro tools are locked for now.

How long does a heat load calculation take?

Minutes, not hours. Design conditions fill in from the emirate you pick, and room loads, plant size in kW and TR, and the report update as you type.

What calculation method does NOVAC use?

The ASHRAE heat balance method, hour by hour for every month, with walls and roofs modelled layer by layer. It agrees with an independent time-stepping simulation within 0.15 % at every hour.

Which UAE codes does NOVAC check?

Dubai Building Code, ASHRAE 90.1-2025 and 62.1-2025, Al Sa'fat, Estidama and Barjeel envelope limits, the Abu Dhabi DoE Cooling Load Manual 2026, and ESMA UAE.S 5010 efficiency ratings.

Where are my projects stored?

On your own computer. Nothing is uploaded; save projects as files to share them with colleagues.

Can NOVAC replace full energy modelling?

Not yet. NOVAC sizes peak cooling loads and plant, and energy modelling is coming to NOVAC Pro. For ASHRAE 90.1 Appendix G or LEED energy models, use a full building simulation tool.
