Heat load

Room-by-room loads you can explain

Pick the emirate and sub-location, and the outdoor, fresh-air and indoor design conditions fill in from the Dubai Building Code, the Abu Dhabi Cooling Load Manual 2026 or Barjeel. Add rooms, and NOVAC solves each one with the ASHRAE heat balance method.

  • Every hour, every month. Each room peaks at its own hour; the building shows its coincident block load beside the sum of room peaks.
  • Walls and roofs layer by layer, with their thermal mass, so heavy construction delays the peak as it does in reality.
  • Parts that add up. Walls, roof, glass, people, lighting, equipment, infiltration and fresh air, in kW and TR.
  • Typical floors set once and repeated, with plans coloured by load density.
  • Plant, efficiency and power with ESMA star ratings converted to the 35 °C rating, and a PDF report and CSV schedule.

Duct sizing New

Ducts sized to ASHRAE acoustic limits, space by space

Build the duct tree from your rooms in one click, or size ducts on their own by entering each space's airflow. NOVAC sizes every section by equal friction or velocity, adds fitting and diffuser losses, finds the worst path and gives you the fan's external static pressure.

Every space checked

Each room's NC/RC criterion comes from ASHRAE HVAC Applications Ch. 49 Table 1, and its maximum duct velocity from Table 8, by duct location and shape: 80% for branches, 50% for run-outs.

With or without a heat load

Load a heat load project from your browser or a file and its airflows feed the ducts, live. Or start an independent duct sizer and type the spaces in.

Fittings and fan pressure

Elbows, take-offs, dampers and diffusers on each section, with editable loss coefficients, and the external static pressure for the fan selection.

U-value builder New

Build walls, roofs and partitions layer by layer

Stack the layers from outside to inside and get the U-value by ISO 6946, right inside the external wall and partition sections. Save it as a wall or partition type; the layers go with it, so the heat balance uses their thermal mass.

  • UAE common materials: hollow and thermal blocks, AAC, GRC, ACP, stone cladding, screeds and waterproofing.
  • ASHRAE materials: plasters, boards, concretes, blocks, insulation and roofing from ASHRAE's table of thermal properties, converted to SI.
  • Typical UAE assemblies to start from, and a Layers editor for every saved type.
  • ASHRAE 90.1 by climate zone: prescriptive roof, wall, door, floor and glazing values for zones 0 to 8, one click to apply.

How it works

Five steps from project brief to plant size

  1. Project detailsName the project, client, consultant and contractor.
  2. Design conditionsPick the emirate and sub-location; the codes fill in the conditions.
  3. Building envelopeCode values or your own, with the U-value builder for walls, roofs and partitions.
  4. SpacesRooms on floors, typical-floor repeats and live loads for every room.
  5. Heat load summaryRoom, fresh-air and plant loads, efficiency, power and the report.

The heat balance method, briefly

Each room is calculated hour by hour on the 21st of every month (ASHRAE Fundamentals Ch. 18): sun and outdoor air heat each wall, roof and window, the heat passes in through the layers, delayed by their mass, and the room air takes it up with the internal gains. The highest hour is the peak. NOVAC's solver agrees with an independent time-stepping simulation within 0.15% at every hour.

FAQ

Common questions

Is NOVAC free?

Yes. The heat load calculator, duct sizing, U-value builder and library run in your browser with no licence, no installation and no sign-up. Pro tools such as hourly analysis and indoor air quality are locked for now.

How long does a heat load calculation take?

Minutes, not hours. Design conditions fill in from the emirate you pick, and room loads, plant size in kW and TR, and the report update as you type.

What calculation method does NOVAC use?

The ASHRAE heat balance method (Fundamentals Ch. 18), hour by hour for every month, with walls and roofs modelled layer by layer. It agrees with an independent time-stepping simulation within 0.15% at every hour.

Can I size ducts without a heat load in NOVAC?

Yes. Open the duct sizer and start independent sizing: enter each space's airflow and room type, and the same sizing, fan pressure and ASHRAE acoustic checks apply. Or load a heat load project and its room airflows feed the ducts.

Which UAE codes does NOVAC check?

The Dubai Building Code, Al Sa'fat, Estidama and Barjeel envelope limits, the Abu Dhabi Cooling Load Manual 2026 design conditions, ESMA UAE.S 5010 efficiency ratings, and ASHRAE 90.1 and 62.1.

Where are my projects stored?

On your own computer. Nothing is uploaded. Save a project as a single .novac.html file to share it, or keep several projects saved in your browser.

Can I use NOVAC for an authority submission?

NOVAC is design software for engineers. Check that the authority accepts it for your submission (the Dubai Building Code asks for software registered with the Authority, and the Abu Dhabi Cooling Load Manual has its own software requirements), and check final figures against the governing code.

Can NOVAC replace full energy modelling?

Not yet. NOVAC sizes peak cooling loads, ducts and plant. For ASHRAE 90.1 Appendix G or LEED energy models, use a full building simulation tool; energy modelling is coming to NOVAC Pro.
