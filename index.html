Why NOVAC

Built for UAE projects, without the desktop-software overhead

Made for the UAE climate

Design conditions for every emirate from the DBC and the Abu Dhabi Cooling Load Manual, with sub-locations from the coast to the inland desert and Hatta that set elevation and daily range. Fresh air is designed to DBC 34 °C DB / 32 °C WB, or the Abu Dhabi DOE condition.

UAE codes and ESMA built in

Al Sa'fat, Estidama and Barjeel limits checked input by input, with ASHRAE 90.1-2022 alongside. ESMA star ratings at T3 46 °C are converted to the 35 °C nominal rating, so equipment efficiency is compared like for like.

Heat balance, independently verified

The ASHRAE heat balance method, hour by hour for every month, with walls and roofs modelled layer by layer. Checked against an independent time-stepping simulation, it agrees within 0.15 % at every hour.

Nothing to install

Runs in any browser on Windows, Mac or a tablet. No licence key, no admin rights, no sign-up, and your projects stay on your own computer.

Results you can explain

Every room peak is split into walls, roof, glass, people, lighting, equipment, infiltration and fresh air, and the parts add up exactly. Hourly charts show when and why each room peaks.

Minutes, not days

A guided seven-step workflow from project brief to plant size, opening on a worked example. Room loads, plant load in kW and TR, power required and the report, without weeks of training.

Workflow

Seven steps from project brief to plant size

  1. Project detailsName the project, client, consultant and contractor for the report.
  2. Design conditionsPick the emirate and sub-location; outdoor, fresh-air and indoor conditions fill in from the codes.
  3. PsychrometricsOutdoor, fresh-air, room and supply states on the psychrometric chart.
  4. Building envelopeU-values, glazing, wall types and the fresh-air system, once for the whole project.
  5. SpacesEach room's areas, orientation, occupancy, lighting and equipment, with a live total.
  6. Heat load summaryRoom, fresh-air and plant loads in kW and TR, efficiency and power required.
  7. Hourly analysisLoads hour by hour and month by month, by component, with the peak marked.

Features

Everything a load calculation needs, in one workspace

Room-by-room loads

Walls, roof, glass, people, lighting, equipment and ventilation for every room, with project totals in kW and TR.

Efficiency and power

Pick the cooling system and FAHU arrangement; see actual and nominal efficiency, capacity and electrical power for room, fresh-air or total load.

Psychrometrics and comfort

A psychrometric chart of every design state, supply-air checks, and PMV/PPD comfort figures for the indoor design.

Reports and exports

A printable calculation report as PDF, the load schedule as CSV, or the whole report copied into Word or email.

Save and open projects

Save to a project file on your computer and keep saving to it with Ctrl+S, or keep copies in the browser.

Undo and redo

Try a different glass or occupancy and step back with Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y.
