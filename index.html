Peak cooling loads, room by room, checked against UAE codes

Enter your project, envelope and rooms. The calculator works out sensible and latent loads with the ASHRAE CLTD/CLF method, sizes the plant, and flags anything outside the Dubai Building Code, Al Sa'fat, Estidama, Barjeel and ASHRAE limits.

Start a calculation Free, no sign-up. A worked example opens first.

01What it does

Room-by-room loads

Walls, roof, glass, people, lighting, equipment and ventilation for every room, with sensible and latent gains and project totals in kW and TR.

Compliance checks

Envelope U-values, window-to-wall ratio, lighting power and outdoor air are checked against the code set you pick, with the clause cited.

Psychrometrics

Design conditions for Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Al Ain and more, a psychrometric chart, and PMV/PPD comfort figures.

Report and exports

A printable calculation report, plus the load schedule as CSV or copied straight into Excel.

02How it works

  1. Set up the project. Choose the location and code set. Design conditions fill in from the standards library.
  2. Describe the building. Enter the envelope and system, then add each room with its areas, orientation and occupancy.
  3. Get the results. Review the load schedule and compliance checks, then print or download the report.

03Standards covered

  • Dubai Building Code
  • Al Sa'fat
  • Estidama
  • Barjeel
  • ASHRAE 62.1
  • ASHRAE CLTD/CLF
