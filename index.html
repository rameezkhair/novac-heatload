Features

Everything a load calculation needs, in one workspace

Room-by-room loads

Walls, roof, glass, people, lighting, equipment and ventilation for every room, with project totals in kW and TR.

Compliance checks

U-values, window-to-wall ratio, lighting power and outdoor air checked against your chosen code set, with the clause cited.

Psychrometrics

Design conditions for Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Al Ain and more, a psychrometric chart, and PMV/PPD comfort figures.

Reports and exports

A printable calculation report, the load schedule as CSV, or the schedule copied straight into Excel.

Save and open projects

Save to a project file on your computer and keep saving to it with Ctrl+S, or keep copies in the browser.

Undo and redo

Try a different glass or occupancy and step back with full undo and redo history.

Workflow

Five steps from project brief to plant size

  1. Project & design conditionsPick the emirate and code set. Outdoor and indoor conditions fill in from the standards.
  2. Psychrometric chartSee the outdoor, fresh-air and indoor states plotted, with moisture contents.
  3. Envelope & systemEnter U-values, glazing and the fresh-air system once for the whole project.
  4. RoomsAdd each room with its areas, orientation, occupancy and equipment.
  5. ResultsReview the load schedule and plant load, then generate the report.
